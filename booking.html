Important Booking Policies

  • Payments are strictly non-refundable.
  • The installment plan starts with a minimum ₱250 payment per slot.
  • At least 50% of the total fare must be paid by November 14, 2026.
  • The full balance must be paid by December 31, 2026.
  • A ₱100 late fee applies for every day a required payment is overdue.
  • Each passenger in one group booking may select a different available package.
  • Cooling Lounge access is open to everyone who avails it, subject to available capacity. Access is already included with the FOB VIP RIDE package.
  • A strict 10-minute grace period applies to announced departures.
  • Minors must be accompanied by one paying adult.
  • A valid ID must be presented for passenger verification.
