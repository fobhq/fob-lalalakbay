BEFORE YOU BOARD

A few useful answers

Does my shuttle ticket include festival admission?

No. Buy festival admission separately through the official ticket seller.

Can my friends choose different packages?

Yes. One booking can include up to five passengers with individual packages for the same vehicle type.

What happens after the festival?

Follow your coordinator’s return meeting point and boarding instructions. Return waiting windows and exact locations will be announced before the event.

Can I pay in installments?

Start with ₱250 per passenger. Reach at least 50% by November 14, 2026, and pay in full by December 31, 2026. See the booking policy for fees and conditions.

Are community freebies part of my shuttle package?

Community projects are separate from FOB PH inclusions. Each organizer sets their claiming requirements and availability.
